## Project governance

Read and follow `PROJECT_CHARTER.md` before changing product, legal content, checker logic, privacy, security, accessibility, SEO, monetization, AI features, APIs, or infrastructure. Keep `docs/data-inventory.md` current when data handling changes and use `docs/incident-response.md` for integrity, privacy, security, billing, or availability incidents.

Before changing a legal framework, official-source record, correction state, substantive-review date, next-review date, reviewer, review-record ID, or public source-review state, also read and follow `docs/legal-source-review-governance.md`. Automation may prepare evidence and blank records, but it may not check human-attestation boxes, choose a human disposition, type a signature, or advance review metadata. Those changes require a completed dated human review record and a matching registry entry that passes the governance check.
